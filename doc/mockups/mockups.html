Chess Analyzer · mobile mockups

Version 1 screens at phone width (360 px). The board always spans the full width; everything else is stacked above and below it so it can be used with one thumb. Updated 2026-11-11: captured pieces in the player bars, no best-move arrow, Resign in the tab bar, game review with move grades and explanations.

Layout on larger screens

  • Tablet, 768 px and up: the board and a vertical eval bar sit on the left. The best moves and the move list form a column on the right.
  • Desktop: the same layout, but the board grows to about 640 px. In the review the right column holds the move card, graph, accuracy table and move list; arrow keys step through moves.
  • Touch targets: at least 44 px. Squares on a 360 px screen are 45 px.
